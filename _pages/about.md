# 👋 About Me
Yi Jie Wong (Member, IEEE) received the B.Eng. degree (Hons.) in biomedical engineering from Universiti Tunku Abdul Rahman, Sungai Long, Malaysia, in 2022, and the Ph.D. degree in digital technology with specialization in reinforcement learning and computer vision from the same institution in 2025. He is currently a Sub-Domain Lead (AI/Vision) at Texas Instruments, and serves as GenAI Technical Lead and Consultant for Quandatics Sdn Bhd and Promptiq Enterprise. His research interests span multi-modal vision-language models (VLMs), diffusion models, 3D computer vision, deep reinforcement learning, and federated learning with applications in IoT and wireless communications.

Dr. Wong has achieved notable recognition in the AI research community, including Champion of the IEEE Big Data Cup 2024 Building Extraction Generalization Challenge, 1st place in the IEEE ICIP 2025 Cityscape Aerial Object Detection Challenge, Top 4 in the LoViF @ CVPR 2026 Challenge on Efficient VLM, and Top 10 Global Semi-Finalist (8th among 10,000+ registrants) of the 2024 EY Open Science Data Challenge. He has published in top-tier venues including ACM TOMM (Q1, IF=6.0) and Elsevier Internet of Things (Q1, IF=6.0), with papers accepted at CVPRW 2026, ICMEW 2026, IEEE Big Data, and IJCNN. His ASEAN IVO project was awarded Certificate of Excellence as the Top 1 project out of 17 international initiatives. He conducted a 3-month research attachment at Université Sorbonne Paris Nord, supported by the 2023 Doctoral Research Mobility Grant from the Embassy of France to Malaysia, and was awarded Best Paper Presentation at IEEE UEMCON 2021. He also delivered a keynote speech at EMPOWER 2025 and serves as a reviewer for prestigious journals including Elsevier Pattern Recognition, IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence, and IJCNN.

My latest CV can be found at this [Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1aOfxLl_cDsX8bfhdI8a6QcaFtP3m4YoAaFsvYwCWE3o/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.gjdgxs).

# 🔍Research Topics

I am excited about the practical application of AI techniques and models to solve real-world problems. Hence, most of my work is in Applied AI, where I focus on solving problems rather than developing fancy models. Based on my experience in competitions and research consultancy projects, feature engineering and data understanding are crucial for building a solid baseline before moving on to more advanced models. 

- **Multi Modal**: VLM, OCR, etc.
- **Computer Vision**: Object Detection/Instance Segmentation, etc.
- **3D Computer Vision**: Point Cloud Detection, etc.
- **Reinforcement Learning**: System Optimization, Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning, etc.
- **Generative AI**: Diffusion Models, Diffusion Augmentation, etc.
